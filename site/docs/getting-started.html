Getting started

gut turns a judgment call into one line of Python. This page installs it, points it at a model, and asks one question. It takes about five minutes, most of which is the download.

1.1Install

No key, or no network? gut.ZeroShotBackend() runs a 70M-parameter model on your CPU. The rest of this page does not change.

1.4Three answers

Every yes/no judgment comes back as one of three values. When you want the third, ask for it:

YES

The model is confident the claim holds.

NO

The model is confident it does not.

UNSURE

Somewhere in between. This is where a person, or a bigger model, takes over.

1.5Where next

To choose how careful gut should be, read Knowing when it doesn't know. To ask about a thousand things at once, read Asking everything at once.
